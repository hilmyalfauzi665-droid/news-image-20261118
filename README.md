# The World in One Day — 18 November 2026

A make.fun entry for the [news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261118)
competition: one image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026,
drawn as a newspaper front page.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run it locally with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## Stories covered

1. **Iran strikes a US radar base in Bahrain** — the Revolutionary Guard claims a
   drone strike on a US radar station, its third strike on the Gulf state in five days.
2. **Ukraine hits the Crimea bridge again** — Kyiv damages the Kerch bridge in a
   missile and drone attack for a second day running.
3. **Quad leaders meet in Manila** — Trump, Modi, Takaichi and Albanese hold the
   Quad summit as the ASEAN summit closes.
4. **Nvidia beats — and shuts the door on Huang** — first earnings without Jensen
   Huang top estimates; the board rules out his return.
5. **Dhoni bows out of the IPL** — MS Dhoni retires from playing and returns to
   Chennai Super Kings as mentor.
6. **19 killed as a Russian plane crashes** — a military transport plane comes down
   in Siberia, killing all 19 on board.

Also noted in the footer: Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's Andhra coast,
Amazon's $7bn renewables pledge in India, Israel's parliament electing a new
speaker, and the US taking custody of an officer accused of spying for Russia.
